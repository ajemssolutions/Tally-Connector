Setting up the Tally Connector

Takes about two minutes. No admin password needed.
  1. Run the installer you just downloaded. The connector installs for your Windows account and adds a desktop shortcut.
  2. Open Tally Prime with your company loaded, and turn the port on: F1 > Settings > Connectivity > Client/Server configuration → acts as Both.
  3. Open Tally Connector and enter your AJEMS organisation name and secret key, then click AJEMS Connect. Everything it needs is set up in your workspace automatically.
  4. Click Run Sync. That is it - your Tally data appears in AJEMS. Turn on automatic sync in Settings and you never have to think about it again.
Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" message the first time, because the app is not yet code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. The connector only talks to Tally on your own computer and to your AJEMS workspace.
